# WaifuDrama — Video Script Structures (7-plan blueprints)

These are the 5 narrative blueprints for WaifuDrama videos. The Thinking Agent MUST
pick ONE (the most relevant to the current news). The generator (SW InfoMissedGen)
MUST follow its 7-row table **row by row, in order**.

FORBIDDEN: the "Sample VO" column exists ONLY to show the emotional job of each row.
Never copy these lines into the script — not verbatim, not almost-verbatim. Fill each
row with YOUR OWN wording driven by the REAL facts of the article. The beat is
untouchable; the wording is yours.

> The sample VOs are illustrative; they map onto a **7+ plan format** (HOOK + ≥4 BODY
> + CTA) with plan durations: 2 first plans = 3s each, following = 4s each. A beat whose
> VO needs more speech time occupies 2 consecutive plans (the sentence continues across
> them); timestamps and the total duration then extend accordingly. One fact per VO,
> CTA loops or pays off the structure, no brand/app/link.

## 1. The "Ultimate New Crush" (direct heart appeal / waifu war)

The spotlight just moved to a new girl, officially. The whole video feeds the
crush: why she's THE one now.

| 2 | The Declaration | The heaviest visual drops with the character + the game/franchise; the designers destroyed the competition | "Here's [Character] from [Game], and god they destroyed the season" |
| 3 | The Design Flag | Her look made it unfair — one specific physical detail | "That [specific look detail] is illegal" |
| 4 | The Personality Flag | What she's like / how she treats you (dere-type) | "She's the [X] type who [treats you like…]" |
| 5 | The Killer Detail | The detail that finishes everyone (voice, signature quirk) | "Her voice alone already did damage" |

| 7 (CTA) | The Rating | Rate her / pick a side — engagement | "Rate her out of 10 in the comments" |

## 6. The "Final Boss Joins Your Side" (villain finally playable / ally-arc)

An ESTABLISHED antagonist (a boss / enemy the fanbase has known for years, often
already playable elsewhere) finally becomes playable or joins your side. The arc:
nostalgic fear of the enemy → the turn ("now she fights FOR you") → the power
fantasy. NEVER frame an established character as a newcomer dethroning the
classic — that's lore-breaking. For this structure, the past IS the setup.

| Plan | Beat | What the VO must deliver | Sample VO (never copy) |
|------|------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| 1 (HOOK) | The Fear | The scariest enemy in the franchise — she only ever fought AGAINST you | "Your worst nightmare just switched sides" |
| 2 | The Switch | The news: she's finally playable / on YOUR side — name + game | "In [Game], she finally fights for YOU" |
| 3 | The Rework | Her own reworked gameplay / kit takes the front stage | "They rebuilt her whole moveset for this" |
| 4 | The Contrast | Her style vs the classic version — the FEEL of her hits, not "she's better" | "No holy glow. Every hit feels heavy" |
| 5 | The Nightmare | The boss that destroyed your runs becomes your weapon | "The boss that wrecked you is now yours" |
| 6 | The Cherry | The one detail that seals it (corrupted ultimate, voice line, animation) | "And that corrupted ultimate? Yours now" |
| 7 (CTA) | The Camp | Pick your style: radiance or brutality — no obsolescence framing | "Holy glow or pure brutality? Pick your side" |
